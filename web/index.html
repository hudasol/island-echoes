    About the data and credits

    Island Echoes is an independent open-data education project. It is not a NASA product, and NASA has not reviewed or endorsed it.

    Where each thing comes from

    • Satellite imagery: we acknowledge the use of imagery provided by services from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), operated by NASA/GSFC/Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). The layer and date on screen are named under the layer buttons.
    • Climate readouts: these data were obtained from the NASA Langley Research Center (LaRC) POWER Project, funded through the NASA Earth Science/Applied Science Program. Values are 2001 to 2020 climatology on a grid cell of roughly 0.5 by 0.625 degrees. Every island here is smaller than one cell.
    • Species records: GBIF.org occurrence counts and species matches. Counts show recorded observations, not population size.
    • Island facts: a researched fact file for each island. Each entry carries its publisher, link and the date it was retrieved.

    How to read the confidence label

    The label is the compiler's judgement of how directly the cited source supports the statement and whether other sources agree. It is not a measured score, and it has not yet been independently audited.

    • High: the source states it plainly and nothing we found contradicts it.
    • Medium: it rests on one source, often an encyclopedia or a news report.
    • Low: the sources conflict or are thin.

    Conservation statuses are shown as reported by the cited source and may have been revised since. Check the IUCN Red List for the current category.
