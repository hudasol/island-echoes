    Data and credits

    Island Echoes is an independent open-data project, not a NASA product.

    Where each thing comes from

    • Satellite imagery: we acknowledge the use of imagery provided by services from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), operated by NASA/GSFC/Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). The layer and date on screen are named under the layer buttons.
    • Climate readouts: these data were obtained from the NASA Langley Research Center (LaRC) POWER Project, funded through the NASA Earth Science/Applied Science Program. Values are 2001 to 2020 climatology on a grid cell of roughly 0.5 by 0.625 degrees.
    • Species records: GBIF.org occurrence counts and species matches. Counts are recorded observations, not population size.
    • Island facts: a researched fact file for each island. Each entry carries its publisher, link and the date it was retrieved.

    Confidence labels

    Each label records how directly the cited source supports the statement and whether other sources agree. Facts labelled High that cited Wikipedia were re-checked against primary sources on 9 October 2026.

    • High: the source states it plainly and nothing found contradicts it.
    • Medium: it rests on one source, often an encyclopedia or a news report.
    • Low: the sources conflict or are thin.

    Conservation categories come from the IUCN Red List via GBIF.
